Privacy

Backwards Planner has no server and no account of its own. Your plan and your templates are stored in this browser, on this device.

Google Drive sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your templates are copied to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your other devices can read them. The app asks Google for access to that app folder only (drive.appdata), not to the rest of your Drive, plus your email address, which it shows in the sync row so you can see which account is in use.

The data goes straight from your browser to Google. Nothing is sent to the app's maker or to anyone else. The sign-in token is kept in memory only and is gone when you close the app.

Removing your data

  • On a device: turn sync off on the Templates tab, then clear this site's data in the browser.
  • In Google Drive: open Drive, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Backwards Planner, choose Options → Delete hidden app data, then Disconnect from Drive.
