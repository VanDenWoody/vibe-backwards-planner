Backwards Planner

Release notes

Backwards Planner works out when you have to leave. You set the deadline, add what has to happen before it, and it counts back. New features are listed here, newest first.

24 October 2026

New

Negative steps for someone else’s time

The plane lands at 11:00, but your friend still has to get off and through the passport queue. Enter those as −10 and −25. Their steps run forward to a meet time, and your own steps count back from there.

Their steps are dashed and purple and always stay on top, so you never confuse them with yours. The total under “Leave by” counts only your own time.

New

Template sync with Google Drive

Save “School run” on your phone and it shows up on your laptop. Turn on sync at the bottom of the Templates tab and sign in with Google. Changes go across about three seconds after you make them.

  • The app only sees its own hidden folder in your Google Drive, nothing else.
  • While sync is off, the app does not contact Google at all.
  • Two different templates with the same name? It asks which one to keep.
  • On iPhone, use it in a Safari tab. The home-screen app can’t do the Google sign-in.

New deadline picker

Tap the time and a picker opens. Choose Today, Tomorrow or another day, then type the time: four digits and you’re done. On a phone you get a keypad; on a computer you just type.

New

Reorder and insert steps

Two new buttons under the plan. Reorder swaps the minutes for arrows so you can move steps up and down. Insert opens a slot between every pair of steps, so you can add one in the middle instead of only at the end.

New

Share, export and import templates

Tap Share on a template. You get a link; whoever opens it is asked whether to add the template to their own list. To move everything at once, use Export all and Import… at the bottom of the Templates tab.

Also new

  • Template names fill themselves inSaving a plan suggests the plan’s name, so “Dentist” stays “Dentist”.
